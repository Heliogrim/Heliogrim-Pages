## How an idea becomes engine

<div class="hg-flow" markdown>
<div markdown><b>Exploration</b>

Options, evidence and open questions. Nothing is decided. Checked against the source.</div>
<div markdown><b>Design</b>

One direction chosen and worked out far enough to implement. Reviewed, not yet final.</div>
<div markdown><b>Decision</b>

An accepted ADR. Short, numbered, never rewritten. Superseded only by a newer one.</div>
</div>
