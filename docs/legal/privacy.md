# Privacy

!!! warning "Draft for review"
    Written from what the site technically does. Have the operator confirm it, and fill the controller details, before relying on it for GDPR purposes. This is not legal advice.

## Controller

The controller is the operator named in the [imprint](imprint.md).

## What this site does not do

- No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or social media widgets.
- No cookies set by this site.
- No requests to third-party servers when a page loads. Fonts, icons, scripts and the search index are served from this site itself. This is checked in the build: fonts are self-hosted and the GitHub repository widget and diagram scripts of the theme are switched off.

## Hosting and server logs

The site is served by **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft subsidiary, USA). When you open a page, GitHub receives your IP address and request data and may log them for security and operation. The operator of this site has no access to these logs. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, a legitimate interest in providing and securing the site. Transfer to the USA relies on GitHub's participation in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses; confirm the current mechanism in GitHub's statement.

## Data stored in your browser

The theme stores your light/dark choice, block/fluid width choice and search state in your browser (`localStorage` or `sessionStorage`). It never leaves your device and is needed only to remember your setting. Clear it any time in your browser settings.

## Links to other sites

Links to GitHub and other sites leave this site. Their operators process your data under their own policies. Opening an issue or pull request on GitHub publishes what you write there, under your account name.

## Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR), and complain to a supervisory authority. For data held by GitHub, contact GitHub. For anything else, contact the operator.
